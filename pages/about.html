About Andrea

A Former Airbnb Market Manager, now your independent advocate.

I spent my career working for the companies that shape how travelers search, book, and choose where to stay — including nearly two decades across Apple, Microsoft, Dell, Boeing, Booking.com, and Airbnb. Today I put that professional experience to work for one client at a time: you.

The Story

A corporate career built on understanding marketplaces

My career spans 20+ years in tech and 15+ years in hospitality. It started long before Airbnb, inside large, complex companies — Apple, Microsoft, Dell, Boeing — learning how big organizations think about customers, operations, and growth at scale.

Then hospitality pulled me in. I moved into travel technology at Booking.com, and later joined Airbnb as a Market Manager. That role gave me hands-on, ground-level experience with the platform millions of hosts depend on — what separates a listing that converts from one that doesn't, and which details most owners overlook that consistently correlate with stronger performance. Today, my strategy is built on that professional experience, publicly available platform guidance, and years of client performance data — not on any proprietary or confidential Airbnb systems.

I spent that chapter helping top hosts in my market succeed — and I noticed the same pattern over and over. The owners who won weren't the ones who worked with the biggest management company. They were the ones who understood their business and made smart, informed decisions about their own listings.

Leaving Corporate Life

Building something personal and independent

Eventually I left the corporate track to build something of my own — a practice where I could give owners the benefit of my professional experience and a strategy built entirely for their side of the table, without a management contract, a commission structure, or a script.

Host Grow Your AZ is the result: independent, revenue-first consulting for short-term rental owners who want to compete with the biggest management companies on their own terms.

"I believe every owner deserves transparent advice, honest strategy, and the opportunity to compete without being locked into expensive management agreements."

Where Andrea's Experience Comes From

20+ years in tech. 15+ years in hospitality. I know how to help a listing perform — because I've spent my career studying what makes marketplaces work, backed by public platform guidance, industry research, and results across real client listings.
