Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Host Grow Your AZ ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and what control you have over it.

Information You Give Us

We collect information you voluntarily provide through contact forms, the ROI calculator email gate, and resource download requests — including your name, email address, phone number, and details about your property. You choose what to share, and you are never required to provide this information simply to read the site.

Information Collected Automatically

If you consent to analytics, this site uses Google Analytics 4 to collect limited technical and behavioural information: pages viewed, approximate location derived from an anonymized IP address, device and browser type, referring website or search engine, and interactions such as clicking a purchase button, selecting a service package, or submitting a form.

Analytics is off until you opt in. A consent banner appears on your first visit, and no analytics cookie is set unless you choose “Accept.” If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat that as an opt-out automatically and never ask.

We have configured Google Analytics restrictively:

  • Google Signals is disabled, so your activity is never tied to a signed-in Google account or tracked across your devices.
  • Ad personalization is disabled. Nothing collected here is used to advertise to you.
  • IP addresses are anonymized.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, property details, and message text are never sent to Google Analytics. When you submit a form we record only that a submission occurred and which service package was selected.

Full detail on each cookie, its lifespan, and how to withdraw consent is in our Cookie Policy.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries and booking requests
  • To send requested resources and follow-up communications
  • To improve our services and website experience

We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Payment Information

If you purchase a digital guide or paid service through this site, your payment is processed by Stripe, a third-party payment processor. Stripe collects and processes your card and billing details directly under its own privacy policy and security standards — we do not receive or store your full card number. We may receive limited transaction details from Stripe (such as your name, email, and purchase amount) to confirm your order, deliver your purchase, and provide support.

Data Storage

Form submissions on this site (contact requests, ROI calculator sign-ups, and resource guide pre-orders) are delivered directly to our email inbox via FormSubmit, a third-party form-relay service, and are not stored in a separate backend database or CRM. If we begin using a dedicated email or CRM platform in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Service Providers

We rely on a small number of third parties to operate this site. Each processes data only as needed to provide its service:

  • Google Analytics — website analytics, only with your consent. How Google uses this data.
  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe privacy policy.
  • FormSubmit — relays form submissions to our email inbox.
  • Vercel — website hosting and delivery.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on this site and receives your IP address as part of that request.

How Long We Keep It

Analytics data is retained by Google for the period configured on our property and is aggregated thereafter. Form submissions live in our email inbox and are kept for as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain records of any purchase, then deleted on request.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request that we correct or delete it, request a copy in portable form, opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do neither), and be free from discrimination for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise any of them, email hostgrowthataz@gmail.com and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You may also withdraw analytics consent at any time by clearing this site’s cookies in your browser, which removes both your stored preference and any analytics cookies.

Children’s Privacy

This site is intended for property owners and business operators. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and, where the change affects how analytics is used, prompt you for consent again.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hostgrowthataz@gmail.com.
