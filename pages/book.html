What actually happens

Fifteen minutes, three answers.

1. I open your listing

Live, on the call, the way a guest sees it on mobile. Most problems are visible in the first eight seconds of scrolling, and most hosts have never watched someone else do that scroll.

2. I name the three biggest leaks

Ranked by what they're likely costing you. Usually it's some combination of photo order, title structure, minimum-stay settings, or a pricing floor that's quietly killing your search placement.

3. You leave with a plan

What to fix first, what to ignore, and roughly what each change is worth. You can go do all of it yourself. Plenty of people do, and that's a good outcome.

Straight answers

The questions people actually ask.

Is the 15-minute diagnostic really free?

Yes. No card, no deposit, no obligation. I spent years as an Airbnb Market Manager looking at listings all day, and a quick look at yours costs me very little. If it turns out I can help further, I'll say so once and leave it there.

What do I need to have ready?

Just your Airbnb or Vrbo listing URL. If you have your occupancy rate and average nightly rate for the last three months handy, that makes the call sharper, but it isn't required.

Will you try to sell me a management contract?

No. I don't manage properties and I never take a cut of your bookings. I optimize listings and hand them back to you. That's the whole model.

What if my property isn't in Arizona?

Arizona is where I know the regulations cold, but listing visibility, photography order, pricing structure and conversion copy work the same way in every market. Most of my clients are outside Arizona.

What happens after the 15 minutes?

You get the top three things limiting your listing, in priority order, and you're free to go implement them yourself. If you'd rather I do it, I'll explain the optimization package. Either outcome is fine with me.
