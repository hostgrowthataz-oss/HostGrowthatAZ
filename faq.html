Should I hire a property manager or a consultant?

It depends on what you actually need. A property manager runs your day-to-day operations for a percentage of revenue. I help you make more money from your listing and your strategy — without taking a cut of your bookings. Many owners work with both, and some just need the strategy piece to run things themselves.

Can you help if I already have a property manager?

Yes, and it happens more often than you'd think. A lot of property managers are great at operations but don't specialize in listing SEO or revenue strategy. I can work alongside your existing manager or give you the knowledge to ask better questions of them.

What if I'm just getting started and don't have a listing yet?

SEO & Listing Strategy

What is listing SEO and why does it matter?

Listing SEO is the practice of optimizing your title, description, photos, and settings so a platform's search algorithm ranks you higher for relevant searches. It matters because most guests never scroll past the first page of results.

How often should I update my listing?

I recommend a meaningful review at least quarterly, plus adjustments around seasonal demand shifts. Small ongoing tweaks — especially to pricing and photos — tend to outperform a total overhaul once a year.

What's the biggest mistake hosts make?

Treating their listing as a one-time setup instead of a living asset. Across every major platform, activity, responsiveness, and continual optimization consistently align with stronger performance — a listing that hasn't changed in two years is almost always underperforming.

How do I get more 5-star reviews?

It usually starts before check-in: clear expectations, an accurate listing, and thoughtful details that match what you promised. Reviews are largely a reflection of whether the stay matched the marketing — get that alignment right and reviews follow.

How do I know if my photos are hurting my bookings?

Look at your click-through rate from search to your listing page, and compare your photo order to top-performing listings in your market. If your best room isn't your first or second photo, that's usually costing you clicks.

What's a direct booking website and do I need one?

It's a website where guests can book directly with you instead of through Airbnb or VRBO, which reduces platform fees and gives you the guest's contact information. It's not essential for every owner, but it's a smart move if you want to reduce OTA dependence over time.

Pricing & Revenue Strategy

Should I lower my price to get more bookings?

Not usually as a first move. Most underperforming listings have a visibility or conversion problem, not a price problem — lowering price without fixing those often just reduces your revenue for the same number of bookings.

What is RevPAR and why should I track it?

RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room/Night) measures your actual revenue efficiency, accounting for both rate and occupancy. It's a better health metric than ADR alone because a high price with low occupancy can still mean poor performance.

What is dynamic pricing and should I use it?

Dynamic pricing adjusts your nightly rate automatically based on demand, seasonality, and local events instead of using one flat rate year-round. Most owners benefit from it, though the settings matter as much as the tool itself.

What tools do you recommend for pricing?

There are several strong dynamic pricing tools on the market, and the right one depends on your portfolio size and goals. I'll recommend a specific tool and configuration as part of a Dynamic Pricing Setup engagement.

How do you measure success?

Primarily through revenue metrics — RevPAR, occupancy, ADR, and booking pace — compared against your specific market and goals, not generic benchmarks. Visibility improvements matter too, but only because they lead to revenue.

Should I offer discounts for longer stays?

Often, yes — longer stays reduce your turnover costs and cleaning frequency, so a modest discount can still improve your net revenue. The right discount structure depends on your market and how much demand you're seeing for extended stays.

What platforms do you work with (Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, direct booking)?

Algorithm, Trust & Systems

How do you stay current with Airbnb's platform updates?

I stay connected to the host community, platform updates, and industry data, and I test changes across real client listings to see what's actually moving the needle right now — not just what worked two years ago.

What is a Superhost and does it matter?

Superhost is Airbnb's recognition tier for consistently high-performing hosts based on response rate, ratings, and cancellations. It matters for trust signals and occasionally for search visibility, but it's a byproduct of good operations, not a strategy on its own.

Can you help me reduce reliance on Airbnb?

Yes, this is a common goal and a smart one. Between direct booking website launches and cross-platform listing strategy, I help you diversify where your bookings come from so no single platform controls your business.

Can you help with new listings that have zero reviews?

Yes. New listings need a different strategy — pricing to earn early bookings, presentation that builds trust without a review history, and a plan to get those first five-star reviews quickly.

How do you handle seasonal pricing?

I build a calendar-aware pricing strategy around your market's specific demand patterns — local events, weather seasons, and booking windows — rather than applying a flat seasonal multiplier.

How long does listing optimization take?

A single listing rewrite typically takes one to two weeks from kickoff to delivery, depending on how quickly I get photos and details from you. Portfolio-level work takes longer depending on the number of properties.

How long until I see results after optimization?

Most owners start noticing visibility and booking pace changes within two to six weeks, though timing depends on your market's booking window and seasonality. I'll always set realistic expectations for your specific situation on the strategy call.
